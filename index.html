01 / The problem

Physical work crosses boundaries.
Its information should, too.

Equipment designs, room layouts, operating procedures, and resource plans often live in separate places. Bringing them together makes it easier to understand what a planned operation needs—and where it may break down.

Our direction is to connect expected behavior, observations, and corrective action. The projects below build different parts of that foundation.

01

Design the equipment

Forma-OSS

Describe hardware and iterate toward CAD models, wiring diagrams, bills of materials, and assembly instructions. Structured design data keeps the parts and their relationships together.

Alpha research prototype. Designs require engineering review before fabrication.

Explore Forma-OSS ↗
02

Plan the environment

Open-Industries

Arrange hardware in a 3D space, animate a workflow, and share the layout for review. Bring equipment models into the context of a shop, manufacturing space, or laboratory.

Working prototype. Visual motion previews do not guarantee physical feasibility.

Explore Open-Industries ↗
03

Check the workflow

CleanRoomOS

Plan local cleanroom sampling around room constraints, preserve procedure requirements, reconcile simulated lab results, and prepare evidence for a separate human QA decision.

Proof of concept using synthetic data. No live equipment control or compliance certification.
